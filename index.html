About PlantAware

PlantAware helps users identify plants, diagnose potential plant health issues, and access plant care information using AI-powered analysis.

Subscriptions

PlantAware Premium subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID account. You can manage or cancel subscriptions at any time from your iPhone or iPad subscription settings.

Privacy

PlantAware processes photos you choose to submit in order to provide plant identification and plant-health analysis. Submitted photos may be sent to service providers that perform the requested AI analysis.

PlantAware also collects limited anonymous product-usage analytics to help improve the app and subscription experience. These analytics can include events such as app opens, completed scans, diagnosis views, paywall views, plan selections, trial starts, and recovery-plan actions, together with a randomly generated installation identifier and timestamps.

Product analytics do not include your plant photos, diagnosis or treatment text, plant notes, name, email address, or precise location, and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. PlantAware receives subscription status information needed to unlock paid features.

If you have questions about privacy or data handling, please contact maximumware@gmail.com.
